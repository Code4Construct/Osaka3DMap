Developer introduction

Turning small everyday wishes from work into tools.

Working as a technical staff member in local government, I often encounter small moments where I think, “this would be easier if only there were a tool for it.” I take those observations slightly outside my official work and turn them into small apps and tools as an individual civic developer.

Public works, document organization, maps, inspections, and meetings. I want to leave tools born close to the field in a form that people who need them can find. That is why I use Code4Construct as the name for this personal development work and publish some of it on GitHub.

Civic developer

Personal, not official.

This page does not represent my organization or official duties. It is a personal space for prototyping, learning, and growing tools. Rather than speaking about grand systems, I care about tools that make the work in front of us a little lighter.

Generative AI

Personal development, once out of reach, became possible again.

With the arrival of generative AI, we have entered an age where anyone can build apps for their own needs. When I was younger, seeing issues such as Excel VBA tools becoming dependent on one person made me feel that using code to improve work had hard limits, and I had almost given up on it.

But technological change has made it possible for me to build apps with my own hands before reaching sixty. I can now give shape, little by little, to inconveniences I have felt through years of work. It feels as if I have finally been handed a magic wand, a little later in life.
